Hexslinger

Hexlings are pouring out of the portal. Lift stones with your mind and hurl them, or draw a Colt Python with a finger gun. Your camera reads your hands; there's nothing to hold.

  1. Telekinesis. Aim with an open hand, close it to lift, push your hand toward the screen to hurl.
  2. Revolver. Make a finger gun. Thumb up cocks the hammer, thumb down fires. Flick up to reload.
  3. Allow the camera. Hands 30–60 cm away, lit from the front. Video never leaves this device.
